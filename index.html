Who Am I?

My name is Jack Morgan, a Gameplay Programmer with a First-Class Honours degree in Computer Science and over seven years of experience in the games industry. With more than three years focused on Unity development, I specialize in building and optimising core gameplay systems, AI, and player interactions for both PC and VR titles.


As the Lead Gameplay Programmer at Cosmic Sabbath on Have You Seen This Man?, I was responsible for architecting foundational systems and leading the QA and playtesting cycles. This role involved close collaboration with design, art, and audio teams to polish the narrative gameplay and ensure a high-quality, stable release.


To complement my deep expertise in Unity, I am currently expanding my skills in Unreal Engine by earning the Epic Games Game Design Certificate, focusing on both Blueprints and C++ to become a more versatile developer.

My Journey & Motivation

My passion for development stems from a lifelong love of games and a curiosity about how they create unique experiences. This interest led me to found Opsters eSports, a gaming organisation where I gained valuable experience in leadership, project management, and community growth. This venture taught me firsthand what makes games enjoyable and how players interact with them on a competitive level.


This same drive to build and create led me to found Wrekin AV, an e-commerce start-up where I handled everything from branding and SEO to automation. These experiences reinforced my love for blending technical skill with creative problem-solving.


Ultimately, gameplay programming is the perfect fusion of my technical knowledge and creative drive. I find it incredibly rewarding to build the systems that shape player interaction—from complex puzzles to satisfying mechanics. Bringing ideas to life and creating a polished experience that connects with players is the sense of impact that motivates me to keep learning and improving.
