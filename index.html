Who Am I?

My name is Jack Morgan, and I am a Unity Developer and Gameplay Programmer with experience across PC, VR, and console platforms. I graduated with First Class Honours in Computer Science from the University of Wolverhampton, where I worked on projects such as Hazard Perception VR, an educational application designed to teach learner drivers how to spot potential road hazards through immersive VR training.


I also developed Mechanical Revelation, my first complete game, which focused on creating advanced AI with intelligent pathfinding and enemy detection mechanics. This project allowed me to explore systems like waypoint navigation, player detection, and responsive enemy behaviour.


For the past five months, I have been the Lead Programmer at Cosmic Sabbath, working on our upcoming horror title Have You Seen This Man?. My responsibilities include developing core gameplay mechanics, UI systems, audio programming, and more. This role has strengthened my ability to collaborate within a team environment while refining both my technical expertise and creative problem-solving skills.

Why Unity & Gameplay Programming?

My passion for Unity development comes from a lifelong love of games and a curiosity about how they are made. Growing up as a gamer, I was fascinated by how different titles create unique experiences. This curiosity led me to found Opsters eSports, a gaming community and competitive team, where I learned a lot about how players interact with games and what makes them enjoyable.


At university, I discovered how much I enjoyed designing and programming interactive experiences. With my background in Computer Science, I quickly realised that gameplay programming was the perfect way to combine technical knowledge with creativity. Building systems that shape player interaction, such as puzzles, mechanics and dynamic behaviours, is something I find both exciting and rewarding.


What I enjoy most is bringing ideas to life. Creating mechanics that feel engaging and polished gives players a stronger connection to the game, and that sense of impact is what motivates me to keep improving.
